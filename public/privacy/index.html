Parents receive alerts about the type of concern, not copies of conversations. The child always sees that protection is on. You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Who we are

CensorX is a child digital-safety application for Android, operated by Mohammad Tabish Ashrafi Raees Ahmed Shaikh, an individual developer based in Thane, Maharashtra, India, publishing on Google Play under the developer name CensorX. In this policy, "CensorX", "we", "us" and "our" refer to this operator.

If CensorX is later transferred to a company formed by its founders, your data will move with the service under this same policy, and we will tell you before that happens (see section 8).

2. Who this policy covers

  • Parent: an adult (18 or older) who creates a CensorX account and manages protection for their child, or for a child they have legal authority over.
  • Child device: a phone linked by the Parent on which CensorX's protection runs. The child does not create an account of their own.
  • School: an institution that purchases activation codes and shares them with parents. Schools are covered in sections 3.6 and 8.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Parent account

When you sign up we collect your name and email address, with a password, or your Google account details if you use Google Sign-In. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them in readable form. On your profile you may optionally add your gender and date of birth; both are your choice and can be left empty.

3.2 Child device

When you link a child's phone we store a device name and display name you choose, the child's age bracket (below 18, or 18 and above), and a link code. We do not ask for the child's date of birth or create a profile of the child.

3.3 Safety alerts

When CensorX detects a possible concern on the child's phone, the phone sends us an alert containing only the type of concern, its severity and the time. The content of the message or screen is not included. We attach the alert to your account so you can see it in the parent app.

3.4 Daily protection summary

The child's phone reports a daily count of screens that were blurred. The images themselves are never sent.

3.5 Device usage summaries

Where usage tracking is enabled, the child's phone reports daily totals of protected time and time limited on short-video apps. We do not collect lists of the apps your child uses or per-app histories.

3.6 School activation

If your child's school gave you an activation code, we collect the school code, the student ID and the student name you enter, to connect your subscription to the school's licence. Your school can see the student's name and whether the code is active — nothing else (see section 8).

3.7 Payments

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or bank details. We receive your subscription status so we can provide the service.

3.8 Technical information

We collect the information needed to run the service: your device model (parent's phone), push notification tokens, app version, and server logs of requests. We use Firebase Analytics in the parent app to understand how the app is used. Analytics is not used on child devices, and CensorX does not use the advertising ID or show advertising.

4. What never leaves the child's phone

  • Screen images. Screenshots taken for safety checking are analysed on the phone and are never saved to our servers or transmitted anywhere.
  • Message content. CensorX reads on-screen text and message notifications on the device to detect harassment. The text itself is not uploaded. Alerts carry only the type, severity and time (section 3.3).
  • Alert history on the device. A record of alerts kept on the child's phone stays there until the app is removed.

5. How we use information

  • To provide protection and deliver alerts to the Parent.
  • To send the Parent a weekly summary report — screens censored, possible concerns, device usage and safe-hours compliance — in the app and by email.
  • To send service emails: link codes, login alerts, recovery codes, device-transfer confirmations, emergency alerts and subscription notices.
  • To manage subscriptions and school activation codes.
  • To secure the service and prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To improve the app using analytics from parent devices.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Device permissions, explained

Each permission is explained inside the app before it is requested.

  • Accessibility: lets CensorX read text visible on the child's screen and see which app is open, to detect harassment and apply short-video limits. Analysis happens on the device.
  • Notification access: lets CensorX check incoming message notifications for harassment, even when the messaging app is closed.
  • Screen capture: lets CensorX check on-screen images and blur unsafe content. Images are processed on the device and never uploaded.
  • Notifications: shows the always-visible notice on the child's phone that protection is on, and delivers alerts to the Parent.
  • Device Admin (optional, child device only): helps prevent the app being removed without the Parent knowing. It is optional, can be declined, and can be switched off at any time. CensorX has no hidden or stealth mode.

7. Children's data and parental consent

CensorX processes a child's data only after a Parent — the child's parent or legal guardian — sets up protection and consents on the child's behalf, as required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The child always sees a visible notice when protection is on. We collect the minimum about the child: a device/display name, an age bracket, alert types and daily summaries — never message content or screen images (section 4).

8. Who we share information with

  • The Parent: alerts, summaries and reports about the linked child device go to the Parent's account.
  • Schools: if you joined through a school code, the school can see the student name you entered and whether the code is active. Schools never see alerts, reports, messages or usage.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (authentication, database, server functions, push notifications, app integrity, analytics) and Resend (email delivery). They process data for us under their own safeguards. Google Play processes payments.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect a child from serious harm.
  • Business transfer: if CensorX is transferred to a company formed by its founders, or to a successor that continues the service, data transfers with it under this policy and we will notify you first.

9. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Cloud's Mumbai, India (asia-south1) region. Some processing runs on Google servers in the United States, and our email provider processes messages outside India. These providers protect data under their own security and data-processing commitments.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account data, child devices, alerts and reports: kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete it (section 11).
  • Device usage summaries: deleted automatically after 100 days.
  • Server logs: deleted automatically after about 30 days.
  • Payment records: kept by Google Play under Google's own policies.

11. Deleting your account and your rights

You can delete your account and data in three ways:

  • In the app: Account › Delete Account. Your parent PIN confirms it is you.
  • On the web: censorx.in/delete-account.
  • By email: write to team@censorx.in from the email on your account. We complete email requests within 15 days.

Deletion removes your profile, linked child devices, alerts, usage summaries, reports and sign-in. If you joined through a school, the school's licence record is kept for its seat accounting, with all personal details removed — no name, ID, email or device information remains on it. Google Play keeps its own payment records.

You can also ask us to correct your information, or ask what we hold about you, at the same email address.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to our systems is restricted, and sign-ins are protected by Firebase Authentication with app integrity checks. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but protecting families' data is the reason this product exists and we treat it that way.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect, and update the date at the top. Earlier versions are available on request.
