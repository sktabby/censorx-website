Delete your CensorX account

CensorX, published on Google Play under the developer name CensorX (operated by Mohammad Tabish Ashrafi Raees Ahmed Shaikh), lets you delete your account and its data at any time. You do not need the app installed to request deletion.

Delete in the app

  • Parent: open CensorX, go to Account › Delete Account, enter your parent PIN, and confirm Delete Account Permanently.
  • Child device: child devices have no separate account. The Parent removes a device under Devices, or deletes the parent account, which removes every linked child device and its data.

Delete without the app

Email team@censorx.in from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm the account is yours and complete the deletion within 15 days.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, email, and any optional details you added), linked child devices, safety alerts, usage summaries, weekly reports, notification tokens and your sign-in.

What is kept

  • If you activated through a school code: the school's seat record, with all personal details removed — no name, student ID, email or device information remains on it.
  • Technical server logs, which delete automatically after about 30 days.
  • Payment records held by Google Play under Google's own policies.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy. Questions: team@censorx.in.
