4. What CensorX does

CensorX checks what appears on a linked child device — on the device itself — to blur harmful images, detect possible harassment in on-screen text and message notifications, limit short-video apps, and send the Parent alerts and weekly reports in the app and by email. A visible notice on the child's phone always shows when protection is on.
